# AP Plastic ERP

Factory and office app for AP Plastic (Myanmar / English). Open: https://newthura.github.io/AP-Plastic/

Records are stored in Supabase and need a username and password to see.

## Parts
Home, Overview, Product, Raw Materials, HR, Mold & Maintenance, Electricity, Retail (sales, payments, customer
ledger, customers, prices, finished stock), Orders, Buying (purchases, suppliers, payments to suppliers,
expenses), Colour Lab, Settings. Every sale has a Bill to print, save as a picture or send to Viber.
